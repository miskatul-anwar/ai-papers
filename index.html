Archival Standards & Principles

Every document in this library adheres to rigorous standards of digital preservation, open access, and academic verifiability.

Direct Local Preservation

All 217 research papers are stored directly within the repository as unpaywalled, full-text PDFs. No dead links, no expired preprints, and no external redirect dependencies.

Cryptographic Integrity

Every archived PDF is verified with a SHA-256 cryptographic hash, ensuring researchers and students receive exact, unaltered copies of the original scientific records.

Academic Rigor & Citations

Complete BibTeX entries and persistent DOI resolvers are provided for every work, facilitating direct citation and integration into LaTeX research workflows.
