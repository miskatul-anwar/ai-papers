About the Library

This archive is a curated reading library of 217 landmark papers in artificial intelligence — spanning 263 years from Thomas Bayes' 1763 essay on probability to the foundation models and autonomous reasoning agents of 2026.

It exists as an ad-free, paywall-free personal resource: every paper has a locally preserved, verified PDF ready for direct reading, study, or download.

Curriculum

The library includes a guided route through 121 core papers organized across 11 intellectual phases — spanning mathematical foundations, early symbolic AI, statistical learning, representation learning, language modeling, and interactive agents.
