Mission & Purpose

The AI Paper Library exists to solve a common barrier in scientific study: access to original literature. Groundbreaking research is often scattered behind publisher paywalls, buried under broken preprint links, or obscured by modern commentary. This library preserves the original documents directly, allowing researchers, students, and practitioners to read the primary texts as their authors wrote them.

Archival Standards

1. Direct Local Preservation

Every paper is stored as a full-text PDF directly in the Git repository. There are zero external redirect dependencies, preventing link rot and ensuring continuous offline availability.

2. Cryptographic Integrity

All 217 archived documents are verified with SHA-256 cryptographic hashes. Users can independently verify that each downloaded PDF is bit-for-bit identical to the recorded checksum.

3. Academic Citation Rigor

Each work includes formatted BibTeX citation records and persistent Digital Object Identifier (DOI) resolvers, facilitating direct citation in LaTeX research workflows.
